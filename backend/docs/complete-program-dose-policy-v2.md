# Complete-program prescribed-dose policy

Policy baseline: `8b0d6e8f1996068755a3573817ac24bd0c7623aa` (the original deployed incident baseline was `0e6494e729a31aa5ce0038af72bc8a36c8967d38`). This document describes the separate policy change, not a deployment or independent release approval.

## Evidence and limits

Calendar authority is separate from physiological evidence. The closed, source-bound `opening-partial-week-v1` contract defers a full week's quota and load floor when fewer eligible dates remain; it retains completed workload and validates the useful remaining canonical source. It does not turn one remaining date into workout debt. Ordinary full-week obligations are unchanged.

An explicit one-running-day request uses `explicit-single-running-day-v1`: one useful canonical source session, with a visible weekly-volume adjustment, rather than inflating one run to the former weekly mileage. Its source hash, input/date window and exact requested/selected distinct-day count must agree. Missing, forged or mismatched source authority cannot waive the normal material-dose comparison. These are transparent preference/calendar semantics, not scientifically established safety thresholds. Presentation, acute/rolling load, progression, pain/recovery and interference validators still apply.

Race removal may reconstruct shared aerobic material up to the authenticated existing canonical program's required dose even when incomplete observations establish a slightly smaller lower bound. It may not use an unauthenticated legacy claim as this authority, retain removed-only race-specific work, invent observed mileage, or bypass the successor's safety/dose validators. Reconstruction produces new validated canonical steps and identity, not a copied approval.

Frequency is a way to distribute weekly work, not permission to multiply full sessions. [ACSM 2026 guidance](https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/) emphasizes individualization and weekly workload rather than mandatory failure. [Lasevicius et al. 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31531139/) compared four split sessions with three whole-body sessions, with similar adaptations under the studied conditions. [Schoenfeld et al. 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30558493/) found no meaningful frequency advantage for hypertrophy when volume was equated. Calendar frequency and muscle-group frequency are different quantities.

Useful is not synonymous with optimal. The [ACSM health infographic](https://acsm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Resistance-Exercise-for-Health-Infographic-PDF.pdf) supports starting with single sets. [Hermann et al. 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40249908/) studied nine single-set exercises twice weekly, not seven-day hybrid micro-sessions. [Rhea et al. 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12423180/) found greater maximal-strength gains with three sets than one in its studied trained sample. These sources do not validate Forge's exact coefficients, floors, interference windows, injury thresholds, or every individual's seven-day prescription.

[CDC guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html) gives weekly minima, not maximum calendar days. [Haugen et al. 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8975965/) describes high-frequency elite running with predominantly low-intensity work; elite volume must not be prescribed to novices by analogy. Recovery remains relevant even without a prior injury. Pain, illness, medical restrictions and observed excessive load still require modification.

## Versioned engineering contract

- `strength-prescribed-dose-v2` uses immutable exercise identities, canonical working sets, repetitions, effort, rest and regions. The eight-set conventional reference retains its existing full family contribution. Sets distributed across calendar dates conserve weekly work. Missing or malformed canonical fields and version deletion fail closed; display titles and adapter prose cannot reduce authoritative stress.
- `distributed-weekly-strength-v1` is a closed per-exercise partition ledger. Standalone strength retains the existing two-exercise/two-set floor. A smaller exposure is allowed only as a validated part of the complete, conserved weekly source. Four lifts normally distribute eight upper and eight lower working sets into two useful exposures each. Seven dates can partition those same sets, not produce seven full sessions. This is a product template, not a claim of universally optimal dosage.
- `running-prescribed-dose-v2` is limited to easy/recovery material. Its immutable fallback reference is six miles (9,656 meters), with an effort-derived 4,666-second reference. A server-selected source fixes the conservative duration conversion before enumeration; candidate pools never choose or update that conversion. Distance remains separately bounded per prescription, weekly and across rolling windows. Equal easy/recovery work receives equal dose. Quality, long, steady, assessment, race and HYROX material retain full-family protection. No aspirational race pace is used to normalize workload.
- Unknown baseline uses explicitly duration/effort-only material. Missing distance is not claimed as observed zero and does not become a numerical race-performance estimate. The existing useful-run floors remain 15/20-minute recovery and 20/25-minute easy prescriptions by training-age class. An unknown or ambitious goal alone does not prohibit a useful plan.
- `canonical-combined-load-v3` compares canonical prescriptions against an independently selected server source in the same dose units. Legacy ordinal ceilings remain on genuine V1 records; they are not mixed with physical-dose partitions. The source is selected before candidate search, bound to context, versions and content hash, and cannot be enlarged by a candidate to authorize itself. Candidate source/pool identity, weekly/daily/stack/rolling workload, actual hard classification and placement must agree. V3 requires the source normalization and exact source-prescription registry; removing them and rehashing every envelope does not opt into a discounted fallback.
- An explicit seven-day request is not vetoed solely because no date is empty. All existing pain/RED, recovery, acute-load, progression, hard-spacing, interference, rolling-load, event-identity, canonical-content and owner/revision gates still apply. An owned event on its exact date is not a pre-race training simulation; the narrow event-identity exception does not waive other gates.

No change here warrants calling a goal impossible from an ordinary easy-run pace. Only appropriately qualified performance evidence can support a numeric benchmark. A requested target remains an aspiration; limited evidence is disclosed as uncertainty.

## Independent-review corrections (failed candidate `91b5845307e9f033320ec061aa06fd854c35d655`)

That candidate failed actual independent Claude review. The following are engineering corrections, not new scientific safety claims or a release approval.

### Fixed source conversion and distance authority

For independently selected easy/recovery source totals `T` seconds and `D` known meters, fix `c = max(T / 4666, D / 9656) / T` before candidate enumeration. Each eligible child contributes `easy_family_vector × c × child_seconds`. The coefficient, source totals, per-prescription IDs/doses, policy version and hash are bound to the server source. Empty eligible sources use the immutable reference but have no discounted child prescriptions. Raw source bootstrap is not a persistable complete program.

Under the **same source**, changing a sibling cannot change an unchanged child's contribution, even when the candidate crosses the former duration/distance dominance boundary or allocation order changes. Adding duration cannot lower any dimension. There is no child or cumulative-prefix rounding: IEEE-754 values are retained; aggregate comparisons retain their existing `1e-6` tolerance. Conservation tests use `1e-12` tolerance and unchanged-child tests demand exact equality. A different independently selected source is not covered by the same-source invariance claim.

Known child distance cannot exceed its source prescription, and its distance/duration ratio cannot exceed that source ratio. Candidate weekly and rolling known distance cannot exceed the corresponding independently selected source. Compensating a faster/longer-distance child by reducing another is not permitted. Known versus duration-only distance basis must match; missing distance is not zero and cannot erase a known-distance prescription. A timed effort-only recovery prescription remains valid even when unrelated historical pace is known; historical pace does not manufacture a distance target. Invalid source normalization, provenance, canonical content, effort or identity fails closed. The immutable-reference path in isolated unit/legacy helpers is never accepted as an unnormalized V3 production source.

### Crossed frequency, phase and recovery semantics

The distribution registry derives conventional set patterns from the same immutable constructor policy: maintain `[3,3,2]`, build `[4,4,3,3]`, taper/race `[2,2]`, and the existing reduced-recovery operation (subtract one, minimum two). This includes the actual build recovery `[3,3,2,2]` source without accepting arbitrary patterns. Exact per-exercise conservation and full dose accounting remain required.

Taper's existing two-session cap also bounds the phase's minimum; it cannot simultaneously require three build lifts. Seven **lift** days, like seven run days, may intentionally leave no empty date; this does not waive any load, pain, interference or recovery gate. Explicit zero/off wins a stale hybrid mode. Fewer-than-seven requests retain a shared rest date where both modality availabilities allow it. Canonical low-dose lower exposures use the existing actual-dose hard threshold rather than being relocated solely because their title says lower body.

Projected weeks retain the original observation date of an acute LOW snapshot. Expired acute scopes remain auditable and become UNKNOWN, never newly observed NORMAL; they are not reissued for every future week. Chronic injury and corroborated illness retain block scope. Validated planning-clock rules still prevent a client from moving the actual planning date to bypass current restrictions.

Demanding-spacing repair preserves a needed benchmark or existing goal-matched sharpening ahead of optional conflicting progression. It removes lower-priority work under the unchanged spacing/rolling rules. The numeric target-pace obligation requires current, independently qualified server performance evidence appropriate to **each** race's distance, using the existing 180-day and 0.3–1.5 distance-ratio authority. Easy/long-only or stale observations cannot make an aspiration a mandatory numerical prescription. Qualified current evidence still requires feasible structured target-pace work; candidate `anchorState` and display flags cannot bypass it. Unknown performance retains the exact requested goal and feasible assessment/effort work; acute safety always wins.

`programReviewRegressions.smoke.js` covers all 224 crossed run/lift/mode/recovery constructors and selected complete mixed-frequency programs, phase counts, known-load unit round trips and original acute scopes. `programDosePolicy.smoke.js` covers distance-dominant sibling increases in both orders, crossing the former dominance boundary, per-prescription distance compensation negatives, coefficient forgery and timed unknown-distance material. `programCompleteness.smoke.js` rejects coherently rehashed missing-normalization sources at the combined-authority boundary. Real HTTP and settled browser handoff receipts are separate execution gates.

## Closed lifecycle and partial-frequency authorities

`opening-partial-week-v1` binds the first partial week to actual remaining eligible dates. It does not cram a whole weekly minimum into one or two remaining dates or create workout debt. All completed workload remains recorded; completed frequency is credited by distinct qualified date, not activity count. Full ordinary-week counts and single-session floors are unchanged.

`explicit-single-running-day-v1` binds an explicit one-day request to its independently selected useful source. Reducing frequency is disclosed as a volume adjustment; one run is not inflated to replace a previously observed fourteen-mile week. Neither authority can be invented by a candidate to bypass source identity, acute load, interference or recovery.

Versioned complete-program expansion/removal retains authenticated canonical timed prescriptions without inventing distance. Legacy descriptor aliases are derived after validation, never inserted into hash-bound executable source content. Canceling an owned event removes only its exact canonical race from the retained-work comparison (race ID, goal ID, athlete, event date and content hash must match). Shared training and other events remain; the unmodified accepted plan is still the material-change and revision baseline. Genuine V1 removal parsing remains unchanged. Validated phone timezone survives preview, apply, expansion and removal.

Numeric HR/pace targets without independent capacity authority cannot borrow the easy/RPE dose discount. Unsupported HR-zone fields fail canonical validation. This release uses useful effort-based running, not a newly invented run/walk fallback or a numerical race-ability forecast.

## Hermes regression requirement mapping

The failed `91b58453` release candidate additionally exposed duplicate material on goal expansion: a retained canonical Sunday long run and its newly constructed adapter had different IDs, so both occupied the source pool. Authenticated retained running material now owns its modality/date slot. This is identity deduplication, not permission to remove a distinct workout or lower a source budget. A phase-authorized replan removes that window's carry before constructing its replacement. Exact owned event dates are available even outside ordinary training weekdays; ordinary running/lifting eligibility is unchanged.

`owned-road-phase-replan-v1` binds a newly added owned road event, accepted canonical hash, input hash and exact old/new week phases. Only an ordinary accepted week becoming that event's taper/race week or its existing post-event deload can use the corresponding existing material-reduction authority. Locks, manual work, unknown constraint dates, foreign events, absent previous goals, forged phases and unrelated ordinary weeks cannot trigger this permission. The accepted plan remains untouched until successful apply.

`projected-road-event-window-v1` distinguishes projected program dates from the real observation date. A currently future, owned, SCHEDULED event remains SCHEDULED in every ledger. Its following calendar week uses the existing one-week post-secondary-event deload from `concurrentPlan.phasesForRaceTargets`, explicitly labeled planned/provisional recovery. This is not observed completion, readiness, recovery or permission to fabricate future NORMAL. The next week selects the next future owned goal. An overlapping next event retains its exact date instead of being replaced by the projected recovery week. An event already elapsed at observation cannot obtain this projection; negative days-to-event no longer imply perpetual taper and retain an unconfirmed-event transition reason.

The projected transition keeps the existing `RECOVERY_VOLUME_REDUCTION` scope, bounded to the derived week and carrying the planning evidence and owned-event projection hash. Taper keeps `TAPER_VOLUME_REDUCTION`. No rolling, hard-day, interference, acute, injury or presentation threshold changes. In an unqualified taper, an unsupported numerical sharpening prescription becomes useful effort-based preparation rather than silently deleting a requested run; qualified current performance retains the numerical obligation and its negative tests.

### Derived-only immutable memoization

The normal dual-race gate also reproduces race-week primary-role selection with an unusable 15-minute easy fragment beside useful preparation. Converted unqualified sharpening must select the existing 20/25-minute easy minimum before accounting, and below-floor timed easy/recovery fragments are excluded from race-week primary material as well as supporting roles. The source is then evaluated by every existing dose/safety gate. A retained converted prescription is not listed as excluded; legitimately excluded fragments remain disclosed. The qualified numerical-obligation negative uses a current synthetic race observation, not an empty-history assumption.

For a result already unconfirmed at the real observation date, the base selector keeps uncertainty bounded to that same existing post-event calendar week, then selects the next future owned goal without changing the old SCHEDULED ledger. Actual COMPLETED/transition-exit rules remain separate and unchanged. This does not remove the existing route-level limits for a requested race outside the current plan window or PR races less than 21 days apart; those remain explicit product limits rather than a universal no-blocker claim.

Each preview/apply invocation authenticates its canonical carry once, then stores a private, recursively frozen material inventory. A binding digest covers owner, input/revisions, assignment/source rows, removal snapshot, request, goals, constraints, target and context; any intra-invocation change rejects before another window consumes the inventory. There is no global approval cache. Hash/stress memoization is restricted to recursively immutable plain own-data graphs, rejects cycles/proxies/accessors/custom prototypes/Date/Map/Set/functions/toJSON/symbols, preserves non-object fallback semantics, and clones returned dose values so caller mutation cannot poison later calculations. Mutable/shallow objects recompute normally. `programReviewRegressions.smoke.js` executes the failed-cycle contamination, primitive and actual mid-invocation revision mutation negatives.

## Source inventory and regression gates

### Calendar occupancy correction after independent review of `9139440d`

`calendar-occupancy-policy-v2` treats the full authoritative modality contract as the request. For validated requested counts `r,l` and eligible sets `R,L`, maximum overlap is `min(r,l,|R∩L|)` and minimum occupied dates are `r+l-maximumOverlap`. Seven means `FULL_WEEK_OCCUPANCY_REQUESTED`; at most six means `REST_DATE_FEASIBLE`. An insufficient individual set returns `MODALITY_AVAILABILITY_INSUFFICIENT` with modality, requested/available counts and the exact eligible weekdays. This is combinatorial correctness, not an exercise-safety threshold or proof of seven-day tolerance.

The failed parent `9139440dd9784348486d4370ba23d7cc89b38bd9` returned an actual HTTP422 with only the empty-date veto for five Mon–Fri runs plus two Sat/Sun lifts, before candidate persistence. Four Mon/Tue/Thu/Sat runs plus four Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun lifts failed for the same reason. Both require seven occupied dates mathematically. The nearby all-seven run-eligible/five-run request with Mon/Fri lifts is different: a shared rest date is feasible, but choosing preferred run weekdays first formerly consumed it. Joint selection now reserves a feasible date before selecting either modality, preserving original eligibility and exact counts. It prefers a rest date already absent from the preferred runs; the canonical ordinary-week search uses that same bounded calendar. It never grants authority from candidate occupancy or drops a requested session to create rest.

The immutable server receipt contains version, counts, sorted sets, intersection, maximum overlap, minimum occupancy, classification, effective week kind, timezone and its content fingerprint. It is bound into the program contract/candidate identity and recomputed during constructor validation, canonical reconciliation and fresh apply reconstruction. Ordinary counts remain authoritative. Partial/taper/race reconciliation uses the existing authorized remaining-date or phase-adjustment count, not a candidate-created forced-union flag; partial opening dates and completed workload are not rewritten as observed rest. Exact owned races retain their explicit out-of-ordinary-eligibility exception. Failed reconciliation, eligibility, receipt identity or feasible-rest placement rejects the result.

Only the unsupported universal empty-date veto is inapplicable to forced occupancy. Source dose, meaningful-work floors, RED/pain/acute protection, progression, hard-day spacing, lower-body interference, stacking, rolling load and canonical/owner identity remain unchanged. Constructor failure responses identify availability, placement, dose/load, safety/recovery or integrity checks instead of presenting every road failure as generic safety validation. Canonical selection failures retain their bounded named failure/check diagnostics. HYROX restrictions are not broadened.

This amendment follows actual Hermes design-only approval in mission `20260909_234804_ca071a`, receipt `FORGE-HERMES-CALENDAR-OCCUPANCY-2026-09-10.md`; neither that design response nor worker gates authorize deployment.
